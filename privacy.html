Privacy notice

How this Walkathon Step Challenge processes personal data. This notice covers the step tracker only. The main club site has its own AIWCD Privacy policy.

Controller

American International Women's Club of Düsseldorf e.V.
Postfach 31 01 47, 40489 Düsseldorf, Germany
president@aiwcduesseldorf.org

See also the Impressum for full provider details.

Purpose

We process data so club members can log daily steps, see personal and club totals, and (when eligible) view a leaderboard for the Walkathon Step Challenge.

Data we process

  • Account / membership: Wild Apricot contact ID, first/last name (last name kept server-side for display disambiguation), email, membership status, and group memberships (to check Walkathon / club / admin access)
  • Step entries: date, step count, public display name (e.g. “Alex R.”), and when the entry was last updated, linked to your contact ID
  • Session: a signed session token stored in the browser (sessionStorage) after login
  • Consent preference: cookie / font consent choice in localStorage (if you use the banner on the tracker)

Legal basis

Processing is based on the club’s legitimate interest in running the Walkathon challenge for members (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and on your participation as a member. Optional Google Fonts load only after you choose “Accept all” on the cookie banner (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR / TTDSG § 25).

Processors & recipients

  • Wild Apricot — membership login and profile/group data used for access control
  • Cloudflare — Worker API and D1 database storing step entries and validating sessions
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static website (no step database on Pages)
  • Google — only if you accept optional fonts (fonts loaded from Google servers; your IP address is transmitted)
  • Club admins — may correct a participant’s daily steps when help is needed

Leaderboard views show public display names (first name + disambiguated last-name initials) and step totals to signed-in club members who are allowed to see them — not to the public internet.

Cookies & local storage

  • Essential: session token in sessionStorage for club login (cleared when you sign out or close the tab/window, depending on the browser)
  • Essential: consent choice in localStorage so we remember your banner decision
  • Optional: Google Fonts (no advertising or analytics cookies on this app)

You can change your choice anytime via Cookie settings in the tracker footer.

Retention

Step entries are kept for the duration of the challenge and as long as the club needs them for that event’s records. Session tokens expire (currently after about seven days) or when you sign out. Consent preferences remain until you clear site data or change them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact president@aiwcduesseldorf.org. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in NRW: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen).

Updates

We may update this notice when the app or hosting setup changes. The version on this page is the current one for the Step Challenge.
